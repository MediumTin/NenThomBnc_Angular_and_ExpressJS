Currently, nenthom.com.vn is a major partner with famous and reputable delivery units such as: Giao Hang Tiet Kiem, Giao Hang Nhanh, Viettel Post, AhaMove, ...


Nenthom.com.vn ships to almost every region in Vietnam. We will deliver the product to the location you request in the order.

Depending on each product or promotion, nenthom.com.vn will have different free shipping programs or discount codes.


Please refer to the promotion program here or register to follow the Facebook Fanpage nenthom.com.vn


AVERAGE DELIVERY TIME:

  • Orders within Hanoi/Ho Chi Minh: Customers will receive within a maximum of 24 hours (Usually 2-4 hours) from the time of order. Except for pre-orders or insufficient inventory, nenthom.com.vn's customer service will call to notify customers about this issue. Usually it can be sooner depending on the time the customer places the order.
  • Other areas: 1-3 days (usually 1-2 days), if after 3 days from the time of order you have not received a delivery call from the postman, please contact nenthom.com.vn for support (If ordering gifts for holidays, please order 1-3 days earlier to avoid overload).

NOTE: In case customers need to receive goods urgently, they can contact Hotline nenthom.com.vn for support

Return Policy

It is annoying if you buy something and cannot return it, so nenthom.com.vn has a support policy to ensure the most satisfaction.


Customers can exchange or return the product within 7 days from the date of purchase.


Conditions for returning the product:


The product is still intact with all parts, showing no signs of heavy use or damage.


There are complete accompanying documents (purchase invoice, catalog, etc.) and accessories, gifts (if any).


The customer will bear the cost of returning the product to nenthom.com.vn


3 Quick Steps to Return:


  • Step 1: Notify us by contacting us via Fanpage or hotline.
  • Step 2: Send the package to us at 303B Vu Huu, Thanh Xuan Bac Ward, Thanh Xuan District, Hanoi.
  • Step 3: Immediately after receiving your returned goods, we will send you a new order.
